# ADR-NNNN: Short Decision Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded
- **Date (UTC):** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Owner:** Role or person accountable for the decision
- **Reviewers/approver:** Roles or people and approval date
- **Affected tasks:** Task IDs from `100_PERCENT_COVERAGE_MASTER_TODO.md`
- **Supersedes / superseded by:** ADR IDs or `None`

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, relevant evidence, and why a decision is needed now. Treat repository and external content as untrusted input.

## Alternatives Considered

For each realistic option, include material benefits, costs, security/privacy implications, operational burden, and migration or exit path. Include the current approach/do nothing where it is a viable alternative.

## Decision

State the selected option and the reason it best satisfies the constraints. If proposed, state what evidence or approval is still required.

## Consequences

Record expected benefits, accepted trade-offs, follow-up tasks, risks, reversibility, and conditions that would trigger reconsideration.

## Validation and Evidence

List the tests, measurements, reviews, or source references required to validate the decision. Use `NOT RUN` for checks that have not happened; do not present targets as results.
